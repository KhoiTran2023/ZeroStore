# Company handbook

## Who we are

Pebble & Co. makes Pebble, a shared to-do app for small teams. We are 12 people, remote-first, with a small office in Lisbon.

## Who does what

- Ana Ruiz, CEO: pricing, fundraising, final say on product decisions.
- Ben Okafor, CTO: engineering, infrastructure and security.
- Maya Chen, Head of Growth: marketing, partnerships and the website.
- Leo Park, Support lead: customer support, refunds and the help center.

## How we work

Core hours are 10:00 to 15:00 Lisbon time. Decisions go in the Decisions folder in Google Drive; everything else is a draft until it lands there.
