# Pricing

Owner: Ana Ruiz, CEO. This document is the source of truth for what Pebble costs.

## Free

The free tier covers up to 3 users and 2 boards, forever. No credit card needed.

## Pro

The Pro plan costs $20 per user per month, billed monthly. It includes unlimited boards, guest access and priority email support.

## Team

The Team plan costs $45 per user per month, with a minimum of 10 users. It adds single sign-on, audit logs and a named account manager.

## Paying yearly

Customers who pay for a year up front get two months at no charge on any paid plan.
