# Sales FAQ

## How much is Pro?

Pro costs $20 per user per month, billed monthly.

## Is there a free plan?

Yes. The free plan covers up to 3 users and 2 boards, with no time limit.

## Can I get a refund?

Yes, a full refund within 30 days of any payment.
